Meet Selznick, our AI creative partner.

We use technology differently than any other media company. It isn’t an afterthought, and it doesn’t sit apart from the creative work. We built Selznick, the world’s most powerful AI creative partner. It serves as a direct partner to the world’s best creatives, so they can find and develop ideas no one else could think of and take bigger creative risks.

What Selznick is

Selznick decomposes every narrative and studies it element by element. It combines that with market data at enormous scale, and with our own study of the individual people in entertainment: their skills, their histories, and the impact each one has on a project. Nobody has modeled all three together before.

Who uses it

Every creative decision-maker on a project. Writers, directors, producers, casting, production, marketing, finance: each of them draws on Selznick’s insight to make their own decisions better.

What comes out

A project that reaches its full potential: a bigger audience and a bigger result, made more sustainably. The same script is worth $90M made the ordinary way, and ~$500M made with every decision informed.

How we partner with filmmakers and studios.

We are an end-to-end partner on a project, from the script to financing, casting, production, and distribution. At every stage it’s the same layering: our creative team, our partners, and Selznick, making the right creative choices within the real constraints of budget and schedule to bring the most out of the project. Capabilities built on our technology and our team make us a more effective producer than has ever existed. That’s why writers, producers, studios, and agents want to work with us.

Narrative development

We work with the writer through every draft. Selznick has broken down and studied every story in the same category, so we can say which parts of this one are working, which are not, and what to change to reach a wider audience. Development everywhere else is one executive’s opinion. Ours is that, plus a model that has read everything.

Adaptation

Before any work starts, we can tell you what a story is worth as a feature, as a series, or on social, and then adapt it into the form with the biggest audience. That applies to books, comics, podcasts, articles, and true stories, and to a franchise moving into a new medium.

Casting and crew

Our modeling covers real people: leads, supporting cast, directors, writers, and department heads. What each of them has actually delivered, what they add to this particular project, and which combinations work well together. We can hand you a list no one else would produce, and we have the relationships, with agents, casting directors, and talent, to attach them.

Production planning and budgeting

We build the plan, the schedule, and the line-item budget. Because we know what audiences respond to on screen, we can tell you which lines change the film and which ones nobody will ever notice. That is where the money is made: cut what never reaches the screen, spend on what does, including where modern VFX and AI production earn their place.

Forecasting

Detailed projection models across box office, television, and social media. For any project we can show the low end, the high end, and exactly what moves it between them: the script, the cast, the world it is shot in, the release. These are the same numbers we use to decide what we make ourselves.

Financing

In certain cases we finance a project ourselves, with our own revenue and budget forecasting behind the decision. When a project needs outside money, we build the case and raise it with our partners: studios, investors, and whoever else fits the film. Financiers work with us because our analysis is more rigorous than anything else in entertainment, so they can commit on real numbers instead of instinct.

Distribution and marketing

We decide where a project goes and in what order: theatrical, streaming, or social. We know who its audience is and what will make them show up, and we run the campaigns ourselves, on channels that have reached more than 500 million people.

Have a project? Filmmakers, writers, producers, studios: get in touch and we’ll tell you how we can help.

From the founder

I’ve loved entertainment my whole life. My dad spent 27 years at Disney. I grew up going to Disneyland multiple times a year and watching every film they made, and I was an intern at Walt Disney Studios in college. Then I spent a big portion of my career building technology and AI at some of the world’s leading technology companies, and watched the entertainment industry apply AI poorly. Everyone is focused on reducing cost. What they should be focused on is making entertainment even ten percent better, and we know that has a far greater impact.

While many parts of the business are dealing with hardship, what we see is immense opportunity. The modern media landscape is so plentiful that there are more ways than ever for a great idea to win. I built this company because entertainment needs an entirely new approach to technology: not one that treats it as a separate entity, but one that integrates it at every layer of the creative decision-making process, so teams can do better. We’re different from every other studio. We’re wide open, we’re accessible, and we have a direct relationship with fans that no studio can match. And we intend to build the largest, most important media company in the history of the world.

— Anuj Kommareddy, founder (LinkedIn)
