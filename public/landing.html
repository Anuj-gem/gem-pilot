Feature films

We're building a new feature film studio for the AI era.

Powered by Selznick — the first AI development executive. It helps world-class filmmakers find and develop feature films that bring audiences to the theaters. Here's what it does:

Find scripts.

Selznick scouts and studies screenplays for our partners — reading each one in depth to find the stories with the potential to draw a large global audience. Built on research across 13,000+ films, it evaluates every script along thousands of dimensions: narrative, character, theme, structure, pacing, genre, market.

Build the financial case.

Selznick ties the money to the story itself. It studies a narrative in depth to understand what, specifically, will drive audiences to watch — then builds the financial case around it: a conservative projection, an upside projection, and what drives each. It's equal parts creative and mathematical: understand the story, and you understand what brings people to the box office.

Anyone developing a screenplay — writers, producers, rights holders — uses Selznick to run new drafts and see the financial impact of every creative choice.
