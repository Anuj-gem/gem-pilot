Meet Selznick.

Selznick is the most powerful AI creative partner ever built. It reads what is in a story: what happens, who the characters are, how it is built, and how rare that combination is against nearly every film of the last fifty years. It knows who is in it: every credit across a career, what each person has delivered on this kind of material at this budget, who they do their best work with, and how much attention they carry going into a release. And it knows the market they are going into. From that, Selznick projects the financial performance of a project at any stage, from a screenplay through development, casting and production, to theatrical release, and every projection is sharper than the one before it.

IP acquisition.

Selznick projects the financial performance and market potential of any IP at any stage. Material that needs to be adapted, a screenplay we option or buy from a writer and develop into a feature, or a completed film looking for distribution: in each case we project what it earns, we understand what it costs, and we make the investment with a precision the market cannot match. That precision is what gives us the appetite to take on projects everyone else in Hollywood overlooks and that we can see have enormous potential.

Production financing.

Selznick reads a script and tells us what it will cost to make: how many locations, how big the cast is, night shoots, period detail, stunts, how much has to be built or created digitally. It also tells us what each of those does to the box office. The same parts of a story that drive the cost drive the performance, and Selznick measures both at the same time. That is what makes us a real partner to a writer. Going through successive drafts with them, we can make every decision knowing what it costs and what it earns, so the director’s vision is properly funded and the film is still made profitably.

Cast and crew.

Who makes a film is one of the biggest factors in what it earns, and Selznick treats it that way. It has studied the performance history of people across the entire entertainment industry in more detail than anyone else, including many who are never on the radar of casting agents in Hollywood. Selznick uses those histories to determine the box office potential of a project. It tells us how much a specific person adds to this specific film, and what they cost. It also identifies the cast and crew who, in combination, given their particular backgrounds and capabilities, are most likely to execute this project well and deliver a big hit.

From the founder

Entertainment is in my blood. My dad spent 27 years at Disney. I grew up going to Disneyland several times a year and watching every film they made, and I interned at Walt Disney Studios in college. Then I spent my career building technology and AI at some of the world’s leading technology companies.

This industry has been pointing that technology at the wrong thing. Everyone is trying to make films cheaper. We use it to make them better, in a way no other company can or will, and a film that is ten percent better is worth more than any amount ever saved on one.
