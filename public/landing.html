What makes us different.

We use technology differently than any other media company. It isn’t an afterthought, and it doesn’t sit apart from the creative work. We built Selznick, the world’s most powerful AI creative partner. It serves as a direct partner to the world’s best creatives, so they can find and develop ideas no one else could think of and take bigger creative risks.

Selznick sits at every level of the creative process, from writing, directing, and producing to marketing and finance, in our organization and our partners’. It understands every unique narrative in incredible detail: what actually makes it different from everything ever made in its category. It understands the market at enormous scale. And it studies people: which teams, when they come together, make something incredible. Every person in the process draws on that understanding to make their own creative decisions, and the work comes out bigger and better than was possible before, reaching bigger audiences and made more sustainably. That’s why we can take bigger creative risks than anyone.

How we work.

We have the best creative team in the world.

We originally built Selznick as a tool for ourselves, to write and produce our own social media content. We’ve assembled a team of the greatest storytellers in the world, and powered by Selznick, we take immense creative risks and make things that others would never make. The videos that reached 500 million people were written and produced by that team.

We partner with the best of the best.

We partner with other companies and creators who have unique ideas and capabilities, from writers and online creators to studios and financiers, and we integrate Selznick into their creative process, so that together we can bring out the full potential of any idea.

And we’re open to anyone.

We think there is immense untapped potential out there. So unlike other studios, we’re not closed off: powered by Selznick, we actively seek out new ideas, people, and companies to work with, and we take open submissions from anyone. Thousands of writers, producers, and companies have already reached out to us. We can only work with so many, and we only work with the best, but everyone gets fair consideration. And we’re just getting started.

From the founder

I’ve loved entertainment my whole life. My dad spent 27 years at Disney. I grew up going to Disneyland multiple times a year and watching every film they made, and I was an intern at Walt Disney Studios in college. Then I spent a big portion of my career building technology and AI at some of the world’s leading technology companies, and watched the entertainment industry apply AI poorly. Everyone is focused on reducing cost. What they should be focused on is making entertainment even ten percent better, and we know that has a far greater impact.

While many parts of the business are dealing with hardship, what we see is immense opportunity. The modern media landscape is so plentiful that there are more ways than ever for a great idea to win. I built this company because entertainment needs an entirely new approach to technology: not one that treats it as a separate entity, but one that integrates it at every layer of the creative decision-making process, so teams can do better. We’re different from every other studio. We’re wide open, we’re accessible, and we have a direct relationship with fans that no studio can match. And we intend to build the largest, most important media company in the history of the world.

— Anuj Kommareddy, founder (LinkedIn)
