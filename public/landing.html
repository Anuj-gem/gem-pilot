Selznick, the foundation under every decision.

We use technology differently than any other media company. It isn’t an afterthought, and it doesn’t sit apart from the creative work. We built Selznick, the world’s most powerful AI creative partner. It sits underneath every stage of a project, from acquiring the material through the release, and it informs every decision made along the way. It never makes them. The people making the film do that.

What Selznick is

Selznick is the most advanced model of entertainment ever built. It decomposes every narrative down to its individual elements, measures the entire market at a scale no one else has attempted, and studies the people who make films: what each has delivered, and what they add to a given project. It is the only model that does all three at once, which is why it can tell you what a story could become and what it takes to get there.

How it is used

Selznick informs decisions. It never makes them. On a project, the writer, the director, the producers, casting, production, marketing, and finance all make their own calls, the way they always have. What changes is that each of them can see what a choice is likely to do before they make it. None of the work moves out of human hands.

What it changes

Every stage has decisions with a large effect on the outcome: which draft gets made, who is cast, what is spent where, and how it goes out. We estimate the revenue and cost impact of those decisions before they are made, so the project is built on evidence rather than on instinct.

What we do, stage by stage.

We work at every stage of a project: acquisition, financing, development, casting and crew, production, and release. Work with us on one stage or on all of them.

Stage 01

IP acquisition

We buy material and we back the people who bring it to us: screenplays, books, comics, articles, true stories, life rights, and franchises moving into a new medium. We also acquire finished films for theatrical release, including films that played festivals without a buyer.

We know what a story is worth before a dollar has been spent on it, in every form it could take and at every budget it could be made for. That is why we move on material the rest of the market walks past, and why we can pay for it with conviction.

Stage 02

Production financing

We invest in projects directly and raise capital for the ones we partner on, from studios, independent financiers, and equity partners.

Every number we put in front of a financier is built from the story itself at a specific budget, with the range, the break-even, and the downside stated. It is the most rigorous case anyone in this industry can make for a project, and it is the reason capital commits to our films on evidence instead of on instinct.

Stage 03

Creative development

We develop material with the writer, draft by draft, at any stage from a pitch through a locked draft.

We can measure what a story is doing to an audience element by element, and price the alternative before it is written. Development stops being an argument about taste and becomes a decision with a number attached. The writer’s voice is the asset in the first place, so we do not develop it out.

Stage 04

Casting and crew

We understand what a person does to a project, in audience and in cost, at a level no one else in this industry can match. Leads, supporting roles, directors, writers, department heads: we know what each one adds to this specific film at this specific budget, and how a particular combination performs together.

We assess every performer and filmmaker in the world, not the short list already circling the material, which is how we find people far outside Hollywood’s usual radar who are exactly right for a project. Cast and crew are where a film’s ceiling gets set. This is where we raise it.

Stage 05

Physical and digital production

We build the plan, the schedule, and the budget, and we produce in both worlds: practical photography, and digital and AI production.

We know which expenditures change the finished film and which ones never reach the screen: a location, a sequence, an added shooting day, a set built instead of shot. Every one of them carries a number on both sides. That is the difference between a budget that buys a film and a budget that buys overhead.

Stage 06

Distribution and marketing

We set the release across theatrical, streaming, television, and social, including the order between them, and we build and run the campaign.

The plan is worth as much as the film. We know what each path and each sequence returns before we commit to one, and we know which elements of the story and cast will actually bring an audience out. We reached more than 500 million viewers on our own channels in the past year doing precisely this.

From the founder

I’ve loved entertainment my whole life. My dad spent 27 years at Disney. I grew up going to Disneyland multiple times a year and watching every film they made, and I was an intern at Walt Disney Studios in college. Then I spent a big portion of my career building technology and AI at some of the world’s leading technology companies, and watched the entertainment industry apply AI poorly. Everyone is focused on reducing cost. What they should be focused on is making entertainment even ten percent better, and we know that has a far greater impact.

While many parts of the business are dealing with hardship, what we see is immense opportunity. The modern media landscape is so plentiful that there are more ways than ever for a great idea to win. I built this company because entertainment needs an entirely new approach to technology: not one that treats it as a separate entity, but one that integrates it at every layer of the creative decision-making process, so teams can do better. We’re different from every other studio. We’re wide open, we’re accessible, and we have a direct relationship with fans that no studio can match. And we intend to build the largest, most important media company in the history of the world.

— Anuj Kommareddy, founder (LinkedIn)
