Hollywood doesn't have a discovery problem. It has a translation problem.

Great scripts get written every day. Most of them never reach the people who
could actually make them — not because the work isn't there, but because
there's no shared language for evaluating a script across a writer's desk
and a producer's inbox.

GEM is that language.

## What's new

We just shipped the rebuild. The biggest changes:

- **Selznick reads every script across thousands of signals.** Story,
  character, audience, packaging, production reality — every angle a
  producer would weigh, evaluated in under sixty seconds.
- **Industry matching, end to end.** When your script qualifies, it lands
  on the dashboards of producers and reps actively scouting your lane.
  They mark Interested or Pass, and the ones who reach out land in your
  inbox — directly.
- **Privacy controls you actually own.** Hide your score. Hide individual
  sections. Take the post down anytime. Your report URL stays sharable
  with anyone you choose, separately from industry visibility.

## How to start

Drop your screenplay at [gem.studio](https://www.gem.studio). First read
is on us — full report, full score, no blur. If you like it, Pro is
$20/mo and unlocks unlimited scripts plus the matching loop with
industry partners.

Producers and reps: industry access is invite-only.
[Apply here](https://www.gem.studio/apply) and we'll send you a personal
invite if it's a fit.
