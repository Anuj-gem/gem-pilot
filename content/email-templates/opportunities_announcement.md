{{first_name}}, there's a new way to get your scripts in front of the right people.

We just shipped Opportunities — a curated feed of open calls from producers, lit reps, and financiers, matched directly to your scripts inside GEM.

How it works:

  1. We source and vet every opportunity — real companies, real mandates.
  2. GEM matches your scripts automatically by genre, format, and score.
  3. One tap to submit. No query letters. No entry fees.

Pro members get 3 submissions per month. We keep submissions limited so every script that lands on a desk actually gets read.

What else changed:

  • Unlimited evaluations — still included with Pro. That hasn't changed.
  • Leaner app. We cut features that were adding noise without adding value. GEM is now focused on two things: evaluations and opportunities.
  • Coming soon: custom posters for every script, even sharper feedback on your reports, and more.

Browse opportunities: https://www.gem.studio/opportunities

Read the full details: https://www.gem.studio/blog/introducing-opportunities

— Anuj, founder, GEM
