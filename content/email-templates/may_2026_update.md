Big updates to GEM this week

Hey {{first_name}},

We've added portfolio reviews by our partner network to GEM — thank you for your patience as we've been building this out. We also made a bunch of app updates to focus the experience around it.

Here's what's new.

HOW IT WORKS NOW
1. Upload — drop in your screenplay
2. Evaluate — full report in 60 seconds
3. Review — in-depth review by GEM + partners
4. Match — partners reach out with opportunities

SIMPLER, CLEARER PORTFOLIO REVIEWS

The whole review process is easier to track now. You can see exactly where your portfolio stands — submitted, in review, or matched — right from your dashboard.

Your photo and bio are now included directly in the portfolio review, so partners get more depth on you as a writer — not just your scripts.

SUBMITTING SCRIPTS IS EASIER

We completely rebuilt the submission flow. You can submit scripts from multiple places in the app now, and it's all inline — fill in your details as you go, no extra steps.

MANAGE YOUR SCRIPTS

If you've got a bunch of scripts piling up, it's now easier to clean things up. You can select multiple scripts and hide them at once, and sort your scripts however you want.

Go check it out — we think you'll notice the difference right away.

Open your dashboard: https://www.gem.studio/dashboard

Thanks for being part of this. More coming soon.

— Anuj
