Hey {{first_name}},

We were floored by how many of you submitted scripts for portfolio reviews. But the feedback we kept hearing was clear: a bird's-eye view of your whole portfolio isn't as useful as specific feedback on a specific opportunity.

So we rebuilt the system around that.

HOW IT WORKS
GEM now lists open calls from producers, managers, and lit reps. Each one specifies what they're looking for — genre, format, budget range, minimum score. You'll see exactly which calls your scripts qualify for the moment your report lands.

APPLY DIRECTLY
Pick the call that fits, submit one script, and track your status. When the reviewer responds, you get specific feedback for that opportunity — not a general overview.

FREE FOR EVERYONE
Upload as many scripts as you want. Every script gets a full evaluation and score. Applying to opportunities is a GEM Pro feature ($20/mo), but uploading and getting scored is free with no cap.

See open calls: https://www.gem.studio/opportunities

Questions? Reply here. Always happy to chat.

— Anuj
Founder, GEM
